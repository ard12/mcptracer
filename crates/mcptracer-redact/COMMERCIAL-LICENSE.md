## When to request permission

Request written permission from **ard12** before using MCPTracer in a business,
internal commercial development or CI, paid client work, a monetized product,
or a commercial hosted service. Providing MCPTracer functionality through an
API, managed service, integration, or paid support offering for a commercial
purpose also requires permission, even if the included software is called free.
Evaluation for an anticipated commercial application needs permission too.
There is no automatic commercial trial; renaming or repackaging does not remove
these requirements.

Personal study, unpaid hobby work, and independent research without an
anticipated commercial application are permitted under LICENSE. The character
of the use matters; nonprofit or educational status alone is not an exception
for commercial work. Rights provided independently by applicable law remain.

## What to send

Start with a public licensing inquiry:
https://github.com/ard12/mcptracer/issues/new?template=licensing_request.md

Do not include credentials, customer data, or confidential deal terms in a
public issue. Ask to arrange a private channel if needed. Other contact fields
are on the About page and can be completed by the maintainer:
https://github.com/ard12/mcptracer/blob/main/ABOUT.md

Include your organization or name, the intended use, whether you will distribute
or host it, the version or commit, and the scope and duration you need. Contact
details have intentionally not been invented. Until you receive an explicit
written grant from ard12, no commercial permission should be assumed.

**ard12 decides whether permission is granted and sets the commercial terms.**
A grant must identify the authorized party, use, scope, and required credit;
fees, duration, hosting, and distribution rights can be negotiated separately.
It does not automatically extend to affiliates, customers, resellers, other
deployments, or other versions. Transfer and sublicensing need express consent.

This repository contains no fixed commercial price, automatic entitlement,
promise that every request will be approved, or obligation to offer identical
terms to different applicants. A donation, unanswered request, or ordinary
support response is not commercial permission. Support and maintenance require
a separate agreement. Exceptions and waivers must be explicit and in writing.

## Credit

Keep LICENSE, NOTICE, and applicable third-party notices with distributed copies.
Public projects, services, demos, or publications that incorporate or materially
rely on MCPTracer must credit it in documentation, credits, or an About screen:

> Built with MCPTracer by ard12 and contributors.
> https://github.com/ard12/mcptracer

A source distribution can put that credit in its README. Private noncommercial
users only need to retain the supplied notices; they do not have to announce
their private activity. Credit does not imply endorsement. Renamed and modified
versions must still identify MCPTracer and ard12 as the origin of the original
software. Where visible credit is required, burying it in hidden metadata or
minified code is insufficient. The license also distinguishes a first inadvertent
notice error from deliberate concealment, repeated breaches, or unauthorized
commercial use.

## Contributions and third-party components

Third-party dependencies keep their own licenses. Contributors retain rights
to their work. Maintainers must obtain sufficient contributor permission before
including a contribution in a separately licensed commercial distribution.

other licenses. Repository privacy and a new license file cannot retract copies
or permissions that recipients already obtained.
